## What's New

**See where the time went, as data.** Analysis lays up to four laps over each other: time gained
and lost, speed, the line on the map, any channel, and the car's grip on a friction circle, with
the video at the cursor. It opens from a session in the Logbook, from Coaching, or in a window of
its own.

**Autocross and point-to-point.** A course with a start and a finish is timed in runs, and its
cones and off-courses decide which run counts.

**Performance runs.** A straight-line log is timed like a performance meter — 0–60, the quarter
mile, stops — with personal bests per car in Trends.

**A fuller Logbook.** Every lap's sector times, gap to the best and speeds. A lap that should not
count can be left out. Each session's GPS is rated, its weather is filled in from where and when
it ran, and what was on the car is noted, so Trends can say whether a tyre or setup change helped.
A session's telemetry exports as CSV, GPX, KML or VBO.

**Grip.** How much of the car's grip a session used, corner by corner and across visits.

**Coaching leaves out what was not yours.** A corner held up by traffic or a flag is not coached,
and distances are given only as closely as the GPS allows.

**Circuits.** Each configuration of a circuit keeps its own laps and records, and its turns can be
named from the circuit's card.

**In the video.** The track map's lap can be coloured by any channel, and a second data file
lines up with the first by a channel they share.
