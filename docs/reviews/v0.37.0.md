# New-user review of v0.37.0

Run on 22 November 2026 against the published disk image, by the process in
[new-user-review.md](../new-user-review.md), in two passes: the walk, and the same evening a
re-check on a fresh copy that also reached some of what the walk had not. Two track days at one circuit were used: the first as
a RaceChrono `.rcz` archive with the camera's chapters, the second as RaceChrono CSV v3 with the
camera's chapters. The codes in a finding are items of [the checklist](../ux-checklist.md).

## Summary

A driver new to the app can get a track day in, be told where the time went and what to try, set a
goal, come back with the next day and be told how much quicker they were — all without opening the
guide, and the first video is sixteen steps from first launch. That round works, and Coaching is
the best-guided part of it.

Four things stand in a new user's way. **The app crashes** when a day's folder that is already in
the Logbook is added again — three times out of three. **Cancel and Close do not do what they
say**: a cancelled export is reported as done and goes on in the background, and closing the
import sheet early hides an import that carries on and never reports. **A `.rcz` archive gives its engine channels as bare numbers**, so the
video made from one has a dead tachometer, gear and G plot and nothing says so before Export. And
**the first-launch window is too small for what it now holds**: Trends is cut off on the left, the
new lap table on the right. Around those sit a set of wrong labels and counts that all come from
camera clips with no logger session being entered as sessions.

## Findings

About an hour of use over the two passes, one evaluator. [What the re-check found](#what-the-re-check-found)
says which findings were seen again on a fresh copy and which were seen only once. Screenshots and
the three crash reports are in the review folder on the reviewing Mac and are not published. The
twelve findings of severity 3 and 4 are filed, each with its issue number beside it; the rest are
not filed yet.

### Severity 4

**R37-01 (#653) · Set up · T13 · bug — adding a day's folder a second time crashes the app**

- Did: **File ▸ Add Files to Logbook…** and chose folders whose files were all in the Logbook
  already (once both days with a folder of unreadable files, once the first day alone).
- Expected: a sheet saying each file is already there.
- Happened: the windows vanished ten to fifteen seconds later, three times out of three (the third
  on a fresh copy, with the second day's folder, which holds no project). No sheet, no message. A
  single logger file that is already there is reported correctly: it takes a folder with the
  day's recordings. macOS reports heap corruption (`BUG IN CLIENT OF LIBMALLOC: memory corruption of free
  block`), on a thread decoding a logbook entry. After the first crash the Logbook held two
  sessions the dead import had written and never reported.
- Evidence: `crash-185313.ips`, `crash-2.ips`, `crash-3.ips`, `t13-01`. In the third report the
  app's own frames are the clip import reading the whole logbook back (`TrackDayImport.importClip`
  → `Logbook.entries()`). Resident memory was 4.9 GB after the two imports.
- Breaks: N5, N9, C6.

### Severity 3

**R37-02 (#654) · Set up · T2 · app and documentation (missing) — a track day's date cannot be corrected.**
The date in a day's header is text; the day's menu has only Remove Track Day; no menu moves a day.
The guide's *Putting mistakes right* covers the car, the driver, the circuit and moving sessions,
and never mentions the date. Breaks N3, L6. Evidence `t2-01`.

**R37-03 (#655) · Return · T11 · app — a recording with a wrong clock becomes a track day of its own,
silently.** One of the second day's camera recordings carries the wrong date. Dropped with the
rest of the day's files, it was filed as a session on a new day of its own, called
*Track day*, with no circuit and no car; the sheet's line for it reads like any other. The day's
best session — the one that recording filmed — was left without a video. Putting it right took
five steps (Move to Day, then the clip's pop-up), and an empty day stays behind. The guide covers a
wrong clock only when a day was chosen first. Breaks C3, C5, N5. Evidence `t11-01`, `t11-02`.

**R37-04 (#656) · Set up, Prepare · T1, T9 · bug — a clip with no date is a "visit" on the day it was
imported.** A clip a few seconds long was entered as a session with "—" for its date. The circuit's card
then says *2 visits* and *Last visit, Nov 22, 2026: 1 session, no complete lap* — the date of the
import. The sidebar counts nine for the day and eight for the year. Added again, the same clip is
entered a second time. Breaks N1, C6. Evidence `t1-02`, `t9-01`, `t13-01`.

**R37-05 (#657) · Make videos · T4, T5 · app and documentation (assumes) — a `.rcz` archive's engine
channels are bare numbers.** The channel menu offers fourteen entries that are a number and *(CAN bus)*, and raw
`x_acc`, `y_acc`. Nothing binds: tachometer, gear and G plot are dead, a throttle bar
reads "-- %", Coaching says *Pedals read from the speed trace*, and the Throttle and Brake panels
in the new Data view are empty. The second day, the same car exported as CSV, has all of them by
name. The guide notes in passing that a sensor is `66569` in the archive and points at **Map
Attributes…**; it does not say how to learn which number is which, or that the CSV export carries
the names. Breaks N2, G1, L3. Evidence `t5-02`, `t5-03`, `n12-01`. Seen in three windows.

**R37-06 (#658) · Make videos · T4 · app — a project made from a session lays out gauges that have no
data, and says the next step is Export.** The only notice is inside each object's inspector
(*"RPM" is not in this data input; pick one of its channels.*). The exported video has a
tachometer on zero. Breaks N1, D6. Evidence `t4-02`, `t4-03`, `t4-05`.

**R37-07 (#659) · See progress · T8 · bug — Trends is cut off on the left at the first-launch size.**
Every line loses about three letters (*…onoma Raceway*, *…p times by session*), and the corner
tables lose their first column — the one that says which corner a row is. Window 1000 × 620 pt,
from the saved frame. Breaks H8. Evidence `t8-01` to `t8-03`.

**R37-08 (#660) · See progress · T8 · bug — a stopping distance of many miles is shown as a personal
best.** *Personal bests by car* lists a 60–0 distance no car could have, beside *No
0–60 mph measured yet*. It comes from a camera-only session, which arrives with **Performance
runs** ticked. Evidence `t8-02`.

**R37-09 (#661) · Return · T12 · bug — Compare With mislabels its first two entries.** On the session that
now holds the personal best, the menu's *Personal Best Here* is the earlier day's lap and its *Last
Visit* is that same session's own best lap. Breaks N2. Evidence `t12-01`.

**R37-10 (#662) · Any · bug — the command-line tool published with the release cannot read a file.**
`onboard probe` from `onboard-cli-v0.37.0-macos.tar.gz` stops with *unable to find bundle named
OnboardStudio_TelemetryKit* on any file, the repository's own fixtures included; the archive holds
the binary alone. v0.36.0's does the same. Chapter 8 of the guide documents the tool.

**R37-44 (#663) · Make videos · T13 · bug — cancelling an export says *Done*, leaves an empty file,
and the export goes on.** Cancel pressed at about 2 % of a whole-project export: the sheet reads
*Done: name.mp4* with the bar at 2 %, and offers **Reveal in Finder** and **Upload to YouTube…**.
On disk are a zero-byte video and a temporary file that went on growing by tens of megabytes a
second for at least five minutes. Breaks N1, N3, E4. Evidence `cancel-says-done`. Seen once.

**R37-45 (#664) · Set up, Return · T11 · app — Close while files are being read hides the import,
which carries on and never reports.** Close is the sheet's only button and its default. Pressed
mid-import, the sheet goes, sessions keep arriving for a minute with nothing to show it, and the
report — how the day went against the last visit, the goal, what was not read — never appears.
Breaks E4, N1. Seen once.

## What worked

These needed no thought, and are what a later change must not break.

- **One sheet for a day's files.** A day's files in under a minute; chapters joined; each clip
  with its session; the circuit recognised; the car asked for where it is needed, once.
- **The return.** The second day's sheet says how much quicker than the visit before and how far
  is left to the goal, in two lines. The car is taken from the last visit.
- **From a session to a laid-out project in one click**, and the best lap two clicks into Export,
  marked *(best)*. The exported frame matches the preview.
- **Coaching.** The corners that cost most, what differed, one sentence on what to try, the corner
  on video, the plan kept for the circuit. Nothing in it needed the guide.
- **Trends' first line** answers the question asked of it in a sentence.
- **The circuit's card**: plan, goals and the last visit in one place; a goal set in four steps
  with the best lap as the placeholder.
- **Undo** names what it will undo — *Undo Change Car*, *Undo Add Bar* — and the car put back also
  left the sidebar.
- **Sync**: *Scrub to a moment you can recognise, then nudge until the numbers match it*, with a
  running total.
- **A control that defers says what to**: *Showing mph for your region, as the data is in m/s.*
- **Unsaved work survives a crash**: the project that had never been saved came back with its
  edits.
- **Export Telemetry** says what it is for and what each of its four formats is for.
- **Name the Turns**: the circuit drawn from the best lap, a field for each corner, two sentences
  of instruction.
- **The Season** fits its window and reads as a summary.
- **Unreadable files** each get a line in plain words: *not a telemetry file this app reads*,
  *already in the logbook as…*
- **The weather** arrives by itself with its source named.

## Recommendations

Written after the walk, and apart from the observations above.

1. **R37-01** before anything else: find the corruption, and until then make re-adding a folder
   answer from the logbook without reading the files again. With it, **R37-44 and R37-45**: a
   button that says Cancel or Close has to do that, or say what it is doing instead.
2. **Clips without a logger session** are behind R37-04, -08, -11, -19 and the counts that
   disagree. Decide what such a clip is — a session, or a clip waiting for one — and say so in the
   sheet; a clip with no date should not be a visit, and should not be timed as a performance run.
3. **Archives** (R37-05, -06): offer the names from a CSV export of the same car when the logbook
   has one; say in the next-step bar that gauges have no data; say in the guide to export CSV.
4. **Sizes** (R37-07, -14, -15, -23): either the first-launch window grows or these four views are
   made to fit 1000 × 620 pt. A UI test at the default size would have caught all four.
5. **A file dated unlike the rest of a drop** (R37-03) should be asked about, as it already is when
   a day is chosen first.
6. **The guide**: a paragraph on a day's date; the archive's numbers; and release notes written for
   a driver, which the release page lacks.

## Not covered

- **Not reached**: colouring the map by a channel, the setup sheet and *did a change help*, Replay,
  two laps side by side on video, a folder dropped on the editor. Runs, performance runs and a
  second logger have no data in the set.
- **Sweeps**: the keyboard was used for the main path only (the Logbook, a project from a session,
  export, undo), not as a pass over every control. Appearance was seen in Dark Mode only; Light
  Mode and Increase Contrast are system settings the review does not change. Labels were read from
  the accessibility tree of the windows visited, not all of them.
- **The drop highlight** could not be captured: the tools cannot hold a file over a window.
- **Re-checking** reached the findings in the table above; those listed as seen once were not
  tried again.
- **What one evaluator cannot say.** These are a floor, not a count: a second evaluator would find
  others. The severities are one rater's, there to order the work. An agent does not tire, reads a
  guide in seconds and feels nothing, so minutes and satisfaction are not scored — and whether a
  driver would in fact guess **Bar** for a throttle, or mind a comparison by weather,
  needs a driver.
