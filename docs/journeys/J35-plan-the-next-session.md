# J35 Plan the next session
*Source: #330 — instructors end a debrief with three goals for the next session, written down,
and open the next one by asking how they went. The app found the opportunities and forgot them
when the project closed.*

1. **Project ▸ Coaching…**, choose the lap to read and what it is compared with.
2. Under the opportunities, **Next Session ▸ Make a Plan** writes up to three experiments, one per
   corner that cost the most, in the window's own words ("Turn 4: brake where your best lap did,
   12 m later, with the same pressure, and see whether the minimum speed comes up").
3. Edit any line to say it your way; Return or leaving the field keeps it. **Add Experiment**
   adds a blank line for the lap as a whole; the minus removes one; **Start Over** rewrites the
   plan from the current opportunities.
4. The plan is saved with the project, and — when the circuit is recognised — remembered for the
   circuit: the line under the plan says which. **Undo Make Plan**, **Undo Edit Plan**,
   **Undo Remove Experiment** and **Undo Add Experiment** step back through it.
5. Next time out at the same circuit, the Coaching window opens with **Last time at Sonoma you
   planned to try…** above the table; **Use It Again** copies it into the new project.

Tests: `CoachingPlanUITests.testAPlanIsMadeEditedAndUndone`. Model: `NextSessionPlanTests`.
