# J62 Find your way round the Logbook
*Source: #452 — the app opens on the Logbook, which explained what it stores and not what to do
with it. The tour covered the editor only, Help ▸ Take the Tour was dimmed in the Logbook, and Help
▸ Show Getting Started changed nothing that could be seen there.*

1. The first time the Logbook opens, a three-step tour shows its sidebar, its sessions and the
   chosen session's detail. **Help ▸ Take the Tour** runs it again while the Logbook is in front.
2. With no session chosen, the detail column holds **Getting Started**: add a track day's files,
   say what was driven, make a video of a session, be coached through a lap, see how you are getting
   on. Each step is ticked from what the logbook holds (a car on every session, a project made from
   one) or once you have done it (Coach…, Trends or Season).
3. Each step left has its button: **Add Files…**, **Show the Day** (the newest day with a session
   that has no car), **Choose the Newest Session**, **Coach the Newest Session**, **Show Trends**.
4. **Hide** puts it away, leaving *No Session Selected*; **Help ▸ Show Getting Started** brings it
   back.

Tests: `GettingStartedUITests.testTheLogbookHasAChecklistAndATour`.
