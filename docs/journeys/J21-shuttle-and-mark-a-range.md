# J21 Shuttle and mark a range
*Source: #230 (split from #106) — the tape-deck keys every editor shares were missing.*

1. **L** plays; **L** again plays at 2×; **K** stops. **J** plays backward where the video can.
2. At 0.5 s press **I**, at 1.5 s press **O**: the ruler shades the range.
3. **Export**: the range pop-up starts on *In to out (0:00.50 – 0:01.50)*.
4. ⌥X clears the range.

Tests: `TimelineUITests.testShuttleAndInOutRange`.
